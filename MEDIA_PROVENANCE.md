# Media provenance

**The hallway.** The walls, floor, ceiling, light and every frame, mat and canvas are drawn by the component's own code. No models, textures or photographs are used for them.

**Preview artworks.** The preview hangs sixteen abstract prints from the shared media library: eight `print-*` images made with image generation and eight `crate-*` images drawn by an authoring script. The manifest pins their stable IDs and SHA-256 values; their provenance and rights records remain in `assets/media-library/catalogue.json`.

The artworks are preview content only. Every download substitutes a working placeholder for each artwork slot, including for customer uploads. No public redistribution rights are asserted for any preview imagery.
